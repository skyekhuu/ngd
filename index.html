About Us

At New Gourmet Delight, we believe every meal should be an unforgettable flavor adventure. Built on a passion for bold tastes and culinary creativity, our mobile kitchen brings an elevated dining experience straight to the streets of the Washington, D.C. metro area.

Our menu turns everyday comfort classics into crave-worthy gourmet creations. We take street food favorites — crispy sandwiches, warm tacos, soft bao, and golden paninis — and layer them with rich, vibrant Asian flavor profiles. From house-pickled ingredients and aromatic spices to slow-braised meats and custom-crafted sauces, every dish offers a harmonious balance of texture, heat, and savory depth.

Bring these incredible flavors to your next event! Whether you are planning a corporate gathering, wedding, or private party, New Gourmet Delight offers full-service catering tailored to your needs. Contact us today to book an event and treat your guests to a truly unique dining experience.
